# Handoff protocol

Work dies in three places: a closed tab, a locked phone, a sleeping founder. Countershift exists to stop that.

## The unit of work is a receipt

A receipt has:

1. Outcome asked
2. What was touched
3. What changed
4. What is still blocked
5. Who must act next
6. Time in AWST and PDT

If a Bot finishes a task and cannot write those six lines, the task did not finish.
