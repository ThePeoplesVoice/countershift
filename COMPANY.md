# COUNTERSHIFT

**One line.** The other hemisphere keeps working.

**What we are.** A company operating system for founders who refuse to let work die when one timezone sleeps. Countershift treats a 15-hour offset as an engine, not an inconvenience.

**What we are not.** We are not a Grok Bot clone. We are not a prompt pack. We are not waiting for San Francisco to invent our idea on camera.

## Offer (v0, 72 hours)

**Countershift Desk** — a public, forkable kit that turns a blank 72 hours into a company that leaves receipts.

1. A locked idea before the other team starts.
2. Three named Grok Bots with one job each and hard gates.
3. An append-only decision log.
4. A shift clock that maps Perth night to Pacific day.
5. A handoff protocol so work survives a dead tab, a locked phone, and a sleeping human.
6. A live landing page and a public repo as proof, not theatre.
